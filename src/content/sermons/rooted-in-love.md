Paul prays that the church would be "rooted and established in love." In this message we look at
how love roots us in God, holds us together as a community, and sends us out into our city.

## Key points

1. Love is the soil we grow in, not a reward we earn.
2. We grasp the size of God's love *together*, "with all the saints."
3. A rooted church becomes a refuge for others.
